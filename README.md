### 💫 About Me

I am building and deploying production-grade **Generative AI and Agentic AI systems** across Microsoft Azure and enterprise cloud platforms. Specialized in multi-agent RAG architectures using **LangGraph** and **LangChain**, with hands-on expertise in LLM orchestration, **Azure OpenAI (GPT-4o, GPT-5)**, **Azure AI Search**, semantic reranking, hybrid retrieval (Dense Vector + BM25), tool calling, **Model Context Protocol (MCP)**, and LLM security guardrails.
<br>Experienced in designing end-to-end AI solutions integrating GPT, Claude, Gemini, and LLaMA and open-weight models with vector databases, Copilot Studio. Proven track record of improving retrieval accuracy by **~35%**, reducing hallucinations by **~40%**, and cutting incident turnaround times by **~75%** through automated agentic workflows and optimized RAG pipelines.
<br>Strong engineering foundation in **Python, FastAPI, Docker, CI/CD, Microsoft Fabric, PySpark, PostgreSQL**, and cloud-native AI infrastructure on Azure. Experienced in automated LLM evaluation using **RAGAS and LangSmith**, with a focus on metrics such as Precision@K, Recall@k, Faithfulness, and Answer Relevancy. Skilled in building secure, scalable, and reliable AI applications with **13x Microsoft Certifications**, an **Accenture Agentic AI Credly credential**, and a research publication in **Springer Nature LNNS (Scopus-indexed ICDAM 2023)**.

<br>Open to opportunities as an **Agentic AI / GenAI Engineer**, where I can contribute to building impactful, scalable AI products and enterprise-grade intelligent systems.
